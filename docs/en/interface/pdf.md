# Export to PDF

The PDF button of the toolbar, ++p++, ++ctrl+p++ or ++cmd+p++, or the print menu of the browser export the whole presentation, one slide per page. Every slide is laid out at its full size once its fonts, images, code colors, diagrams and formulas are loaded, then the print dialog of the browser opens: choose **Save as PDF**.

Use Chrome or Firefox, which print pages at the exact size of the slides. The text stays vector: sharp at any zoom, selectable and searchable, and links keep working.

## What the PDF shows

- Each slide with all its steps. A presentation can print one page per step instead, see [`printSteps`](../building/slides.md#presentation).
- Videos as their first frame or their poster, with a play sign, linking to the video.
- Gradient texts letter by letter and blurred shadows as sharp layers: they look the same in every PDF reader, Preview on macOS included.
