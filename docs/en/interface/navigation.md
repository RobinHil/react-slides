# Moving through slides

## With the toolbar

![The toolbar](../images/interface/toolbar.png)

The navigation, in the middle of the toolbar, goes to the first slide, the previous one, the next one and the last slide. Between them, the number of the current slide can be typed over: type a number, then ++enter++ or leave the field to go there, ++esc++ to cancel.

## With the keyboard

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ++arrow-right++ or ++page-down++ | Next step, then next slide |
| ++arrow-left++ or ++page-up++ | Previous step, then previous slide |
| ++arrow-down++ ++arrow-up++ | First and last slide |
| ++0++ to ++9++ | Type a slide number, then ++enter++ |

++page-up++ and ++page-down++ are what presentation remotes send: a remote works out of the box.

## Steps

Some slides reveal their content one piece at a time. The next arrow then shows the next piece before moving to the next slide, and the previous arrow hides them again. Going back to such a slide shows it whole, as it was left.

The progress bar at the bottom moves at each step, and the address records it: `#20.2` is the second step of slide 20.

## On a touch screen

Swipe left for the next slide, right for the previous one. In full screen, tap the slide to show or hide the toolbar.

## Transitions

Moving to the next or previous slide plays a short transition, a fade or a slide, depending on the presentation. Jumping to a slide from its number, the slide list or the mosaic is instant. Transitions are left out when the system asks for less motion.
